Activity

The location journey from the weather page. Successful branches end on that place's weather.

flowchart TD
    accTitle: Location journey
    accDescr: From the weather page, device location, city search, or a postal code and country leads to that place's weather, or to a refusal or no-match message.
    Start(["Weather page"]) --> Choice{"How is the place chosen?"}
    Choice --> Device["Use my location"]
    Choice --> City["City search"]
    Choice --> Postal["Postal code and country"]

    Device --> Asking["Finding your location…"]
    Asking --> Allowed{"Location available?"}
    Allowed -->|Allowed| DevicePlace["Load that place"]
    Allowed -->|Refused or unavailable| Refused["Short note. Use my location stays available."]
    DevicePlace --> Weather(["That place's weather"])

    City --> CityResult{"How many cities match?"}
    CityResult -->|None| NoCity["No match. The current place stays."]
    CityResult -->|One| OneCity["Open that place"]
    CityResult -->|Two to five| Choose["List city, region when present, and country"]
    Choose --> Picked["Person chooses one"]
    OneCity --> Weather
    Picked --> Weather

    Postal --> PostalResult{"A place for that postal code?"}
    PostalResult -->|One| PostalPlace["Open that place"]
    PostalResult -->|None| NoPostal["No matching place"]
    PostalPlace --> Weather
